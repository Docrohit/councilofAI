# Measure Council performance

Concern: [latency/context/usage/DB/rendering]. Reproduce a baseline and distinguish
measurement from estimates. Bound authorized model costs and avoid active user
projects. Measure relevant model/queue/tool time, total context, SQLite work,
SSE replay, rendering and coordination repair. Record model/config/task versions.

Compare Council and single agents for quality and efficiency separately, with
comparable tools and stated budgets. Include tokens, latency, known cost,
repeated attempts and failures. Equal calls aren't equal cost; shorter wrong
answers aren't improvements.

If authorized, fix the measured bottleneck and verify correctness and the same
metric. Don't drop objections, evidence or approvals for speed. Obtain review
and report costs/trade-offs, not just favorable numbers.
